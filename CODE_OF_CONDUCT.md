# MinkExcel Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want MinkExcel to be a welcoming community where everyone can participate
without harassment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to follow this code of
conduct, including contributors and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and consider different perspectives and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, and accept feedback graciously.
- Use welcoming language and help newcomers participate.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and adjust your
  behavior when it causes harm.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and respect other people's boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive discussions.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or helps with an investigation.
- Other behavior that undermines a safe and respectful community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project communication channels. It also applies when
someone represents MinkExcel in public, such as through an official project
account or at an event.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer using a contact method listed
on their GitHub profile. Maintainers can be identified through the
[repository](https://github.com/accntech/minkexcel) and its recent merged pull
requests. If no private contact method is available, open an issue asking for a
private reporting channel without sharing details of the incident or identifying
the people involved.

Include the relevant links, what happened, when it happened, and any information
that would help maintainers understand the situation. Share only the personal
information needed to handle the report. If a concern involves a maintainer,
contact another maintainer where possible.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and handle them as confidentially as
possible. Information will be shared only with people who need it to investigate
or respond. Do not post confidential reports in public issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code consistently. They may
remove or edit content that violates it and will consider the severity, impact,
and any pattern of behavior when deciding how to respond.

Depending on the circumstances, a response may include:

1. A private correction explaining the behavior and the expected change.
2. A warning with clear conditions for continued participation.
3. A temporary restriction from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Where possible, maintainers
will explain the decision and its duration to the person affected. Anyone who
believes a decision was made in error may request reconsideration privately,
providing relevant context or new information.
